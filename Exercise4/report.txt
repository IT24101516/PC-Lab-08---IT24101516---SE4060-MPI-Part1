Lab 8 - Exercise 4
The PDF refers to Exercises 1 and 2, but Exercise 1 is repository setup and has no computational workload. Therefore benchmark both computational exercises: Exercise 2 (sum) and Exercise 3 (Monte Carlo pi). Also compare Exercises 6 and 7.
Use MPI_Wtime, synchronized start, and the maximum elapsed duration across ranks. Times cover computation plus result collection; MPI startup is excluded. Take five measurements per process count and plot medians. Speedup is median T(1) / median T(p). Very small sum workloads may be dominated by communication and measurement overhead.
run_lab.sh writes actual timings.csv and hardware/environment information. plot_results.py checks sums and identical pi counts before creating time.png, speedup.png and summary.csv.
Single-host process scaling is not multi-node scaling. Record the host configuration used. Do not label oversubscribed process counts as additional physical processors.
Completed 60 timed EC2 runs: four programs, three process counts (1, 2, 4), five repetitions each. Graphs: time.png and speedup.png. Hardware and launch flags: environment.txt. One available CPU on one node means 2 and 4 ranks were oversubscribed; these results cannot establish multi-node scaling.
